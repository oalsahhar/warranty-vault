How It Works

  1. Save: Add your product with its invoice details.
  2. Track: See when each warranty ends and which ones expire soon.
  3. Claim: Describe the problem and get a ready claim email.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the site send the claim to the company?

No. The site prepares the message and opens your email app, and you send it yourself with the invoice attached.

Where do the company emails come from?

From a list of companies saved in the system, taken from their official contact pages.

What if I lose the paper invoice?

Save the order number and store name. The store can usually give you a copy.
