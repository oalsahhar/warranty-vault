About Us

Why Warranty Vault?

Many people lose their warranty rights, not because the product works fine, but because claiming is hard. Paper invoices fade or get lost, warranties expire without anyone noticing, and people do not know whether to contact the brand or the store.

Warranty Vault keeps every warranty in one place. You save each product with its invoice details, the site shows when each warranty ends, and when something breaks it prepares a ready claim message with the right company contact.

What You Can Do

  • Save your products with the store, order number, and purchase date.
  • See which warranties are active, expiring soon, or expired.
  • Get a ready claim email for repair, replacement, or refund.
